title: "claude.ai"
description: "Add Alpha Insight as a custom connector in claude.ai using OAuth."
---

claude.ai connects to Alpha Insight with OAuth 2.1. There is no key to copy: you approve the
connection once on a consent screen, and claude.ai keeps the tokens fresh on its own.

## Add the connector

1. In claude.ai, open **Settings → Connectors** and click **Add custom connector**.
2. Enter a name, for example `Alpha Insight`.
3. For the URL, enter:

   ```text
   https://api.alphainsight.xyz/mcp
   ```

4. Save. claude.ai reads the server's discovery documents, registers itself, and opens the Alpha
   Insight consent page in your browser.

## Approve the connection

On the consent page you sign in with your Alpha Insight account, then click **Allow**.

The page shows the app's name, the host it will send you back to, and exactly what it will be able
to do. Each permission is listed with its own sentence and its raw name, so nothing is hidden:

| Permission | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `data:read` | See market data and your analytics. |
| `trade:read` | See your balances, orders and fills. |
| `trade:write` | Place, change and cancel orders with your money. It can never withdraw. |

The page lists only the permissions the app requested. If the connection holds `trade:write`, the
page also says it can never withdraw.

<Note>
`trade:write` is one of the permissions Alpha Insight can grant, but no tool is behind it today.
Every current tool works with `data:read` except `get_portfolio_summary`, which needs `trade:read`.
See [Permissions](/auth/scopes).
</Note>

After you allow it, claude.ai finishes the connection and its tools appear in your chats.

## Disconnect

You can disconnect from either side:

- In claude.ai, remove the connector from **Settings → Connectors**.
- In Alpha Insight, open **Settings → Connected Apps** and click **Disconnect**.

Disconnecting revokes the app's tokens immediately. See [OAuth](/auth/oauth) for the full flow
and how grants are listed.

## Troubleshooting

If the browser never reaches the consent page, check that the URL is exactly
`https://api.alphainsight.xyz/mcp` and that you are signed in to Alpha Insight in the same browser.
If a consent request has expired, start the connection again from claude.ai — requests are
single-use.
